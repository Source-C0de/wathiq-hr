# Nitaqat Bands — Saudi Saudization Compliance Program

Nitaqat is the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD)
program that classifies private-sector establishments in Saudi Arabia into
bands based on their percentage of Saudi nationals in their workforce. The
program was launched in 2011 and recalibrated several times since, most
recently in 2024.

## How the bands are calculated

The band of an establishment is determined by two factors:

1. **Saudization ratio** — the percentage of the total workforce that is
   Saudi. The required ratio is set by industry and establishment size,
   with smaller establishments and certain industries having lower
   thresholds.

2. **Saudization performance band** — each industry has a target ratio.
   Establishments that meet or exceed the target fall into the higher
   bands; establishments that fall below fall into the lower bands.

The bands, from highest to lowest, are:

- **Platinum** — top-tier compliance. Establishments enjoy the maximum
  freedom in hiring expatriate workers, including instant work-permit
  issuance and unlimited transfers of sponsorship.
- **Green (High)** — strong compliance. Establishments can hire
  expatriates and have relaxed access to MHRSD services.
- **Green (Low)** — borderline compliance. Establishments face limits on
  new expatriate work permits.
- **Yellow** — below target. Establishments can renew existing expatriate
  workers but cannot issue new work permits or transfer sponsorships.
- **Red** — significantly below target. Establishments cannot renew
  expatriate visas, cannot transfer sponsorship, and face escalating
  fines.

## How to compute the Saudization ratio

The ratio is calculated as:

> (Number of Saudi workers) / (Total number of workers) × 100

"Numerator" adjustments exist for certain categories (e.g. part-time Saudi
workers, Saudi students in co-op programs, and some Tawteen-program
participation). "Denominator" adjustments exist for high-skill expatriate
positions and for workers in remote areas.

## What is the "Platinum" Nitaqat band?

Platinum is the highest band, reserved for establishments that meet or
exceed the industry target by a significant margin (typically 1.5x to 2x
the target, depending on industry). Platinum-band establishments enjoy:

1. **Instant work permit issuance.** Other bands have to wait for batch
   approvals; Platinum establishments can issue work permits in real time.
2. **Unlimited sponsor transfers.** Workers can transfer to a Platinum
   employer without any MHRSD pre-approval.
3. **Freedom to change professions.** Workers can change their job title
   on their Iqama without waiting for quotas.
4. **Reduced inspection frequency.** Platinum establishments are exempt
   from routine MHRSD inspections and are subject only to complaint-driven
   inspections.
5. **Priority access to government tenders.** Some government procurement
   processes award additional points to Platinum-band establishments.

Achieving Platinum is therefore both a compliance milestone and a
competitive advantage — it expands the establishment's hiring pool and
reduces operational friction.
