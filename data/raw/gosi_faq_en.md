# GOSI — General Organization for Social Insurance (FAQ Summary)

The General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) administers two main
branches of insurance in Saudi Arabia:

1. **Occupational Hazards Branch** — covers work-related injuries and
   occupational diseases for all workers in the private sector.
2. **Anonymity / Pensions Branch** — covers old age, disability, and death
   for Saudi workers, and the occupational hazards branch for non-Saudi
   workers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What is GOSI?**
GOSI is the Saudi government body that provides social insurance coverage to
private-sector workers, including occupational hazards, pensions, and
unemployment insurance (SANED). It is funded by contributions from employers,
employees (where applicable), and the government.

**Q2. Which workers are covered?**
All private-sector workers in Saudi Arabia, both Saudi and non-Saudi, are
covered by the **occupational hazards branch**. Saudi workers are additionally
covered by the **pensions branch** and the **unemployment insurance (SANED)**
scheme. Domestic workers, the self-employed, and some other categories have
their own registration pathways.

**Q3. Are domestic workers covered by GOSI?**
Yes. Since 2022 amendments, domestic workers are required to be registered
with GOSI under the occupational hazards branch. Their employer (the sponsor)
is responsible for paying the GOSI contributions.

**Q4. How does an employer register a new employee with GOSI?**
The employer registers the employee through the GOSI online portal
(gosi.gov.sa) or the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development
(MHRSD) e-services. The required steps are:

1. Log in to the GOSI portal using the employer's credentials.
2. Add the new employee with their Iqama / National ID, salary, and date of
   joining.
3. Verify the employee's data (the employee will receive an SMS to confirm).
4. The system generates a monthly contribution invoice based on the
   declared salary.

The employer must register the employee within 15 days of the employment
start date. Late registration may attract fines.

**Q5. What is the contribution rate?**
The contribution rate depends on the branch and the worker's nationality:

- **Occupational hazards:** 2 percent of the monthly contributory wage, paid
  by the employer.
- **Pensions (Saudi only):** 9 percent of the monthly contributory wage, paid
  by the employer, plus 9 percent paid by the employee.
- **SANED (unemployment, Saudi only):** 0.75 percent by the employer, 0.75
  percent by the employee.

The maximum contributory wage is currently SAR 45,000 per month (subject to
periodic updates).

**Q6. What benefits does a worker receive from GOSI?**
- Occupational injury compensation, including medical care and a disability
  pension where applicable.
- A retirement pension at age 60 (with at least 120 months of contributions)
  for Saudi workers.
- A lump-sum payment if the worker leaves employment before retirement age
  and has between 12 and 120 months of contributions.
- Survivor benefits for the family in case of death.

**Q7. Can the worker check their GOSI contribution history?**
Yes, via the GOSI portal or app, using the national ID / Iqama and a one-time
password. The portal shows the contributions made by the employer each
month, the running total, and the estimated pension.

**Q8. What if the employer fails to register the worker or pay GOSI
contributions?**
The worker can file a complaint with the MHRSD. If the employer is found to
have failed to register or pay, they are liable for back-payment of all
contributions plus fines, and the worker is credited with the missing
contribution period for the purpose of pension calculation.
